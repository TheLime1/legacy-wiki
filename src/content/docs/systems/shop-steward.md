The first **Transcend** unlocks the Shop Steward at the top of the Shop. It recommends a property and set of miscellaneous possessions for the class, primary ability, and Echo targets currently being trained.

## Fee and budget

While enabled, the Steward charges **10% of gross class income** as a daily expense. Its recommendation must fit within the remaining 90%:

$$
\operatorname{budget/day}=0.9\times\operatorname{income/day}.
$$

The Steward compares the useful multipliers that affect the current training set, then chooses the strongest affordable combination. It refreshes the recommendation every 30 active in-game days. A new unlock or an unaffordable recommendation also causes a recalculation when you try to apply it.

## Controls

1. **Enable** the Steward to begin paying the fee and create a recommendation.
2. Review its listed property, possessions, expense, and budget.
3. Select **Buy loadout** to apply the recommendation manually.

The Steward never buys a recommendation automatically. A manual Shop change means the recommended loadout is no longer fully applied, but recommendations continue refreshing while the Steward remains enabled. Rebirth removes active possessions without removing the permanent Steward unlock or enabled choice.

Disabling the Steward stops its fee and clears every active Shop item, returning the property to Homeless.
