All three paths begin the next life at age 14 with zero copper, Beggar, Concentration, and Homeless. Current task levels and XP are cleared, as are the selected property and active miscellaneous possessions. Mastery discoveries, class worked peaks, tiers, and passive effects survive every normal rebirth.

## Touch Eye

Available at age 65. For each task, it records the greater of current level and previous maximum level. The next-life task-specific XP multiplier is `1 + maximum level / 10`.

This task-specific acceleration is separate from mastery. A mastered class keeps its own-class and secondary item effects even in a fresh life.

## Embrace Evil

Available at age 200. It adds the displayed Evil gain, then clears every recorded maximum level. Completing it once unlocks the option for auto-promote to cross class-group boundaries.

## Transcend

Available at Cosmic Recollection level 1. It awards `Yin Yang effect × Essence Collector effect` Essence, multiplied by 1.5 when the pre-award Essence total is at least 30,000. Evil is cleared. Each recorded maximum becomes:

`floor(Cosmic Recollection effect × max(current level, recorded maximum))`

Rebirth counts no longer grant a capped global XP multiplier. They retain all of their other uses. Achievements, Essence, permanent unlocks, mastery progress, and enabled automation choices survive unless a path explicitly resets them.

See [Mastery Items](../mastery-items/) for the independent worked-peak formula, all 35 items, and their fresh-life effects.
