**Eternal Ascension** is the complete expansion from Echo I through the permanent ending. It adds four Echoes, Great promotions, Collapse, Darkness, the Metaverse, 42 milestones, nine classes, ten abilities, twenty possessions, and nine Mastery items.

## Progression path

| Turning point                                          | Main unlocks                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| First Embrace Evil                                     | Echo I, Resonance I, Evil Perk Points, and cross-career automation |
| First Transcend                                        | Echo II, Resonance II, and the Shop Steward                        |
| 5,000,000 Essence and One Above All peak 2,000         | Great task promotions and Great item conversions                   |
| Heroic XIV at 50,000,000,000 Essence                   | Collapse and Dark Matter                                           |
| First Collapse                                         | Echo III and Resonance III                                         |
| Darkness XIII at 1,000,000 total Dark Matter           | Metaverse entry and Metaverse Points                               |
| First Metaverse entry                                  | Echo IV and Resonance IV                                           |
| Metaverse VI and Great The Unwritten Absolute level 20 | Permanent Legacy Complete ending                                   |

Progress is reinforced by four milestone families: [Essence, Heroic, Darkness, and Metaverse](../chronicle/). Completed milestones, Great promotion status, mastery progress, Resonance memories, Evil perks, lifetime currencies, and Chronicle records persist through the later resets unless a system guide explicitly says otherwise.

Continue with [Heroic Progression](../heroic/), [Darkness and Collapse](../darkness/), and [The Metaverse](../metaverse/) for exact formulas and reset rules.
