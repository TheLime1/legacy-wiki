Legacy has 30 properties and 24 miscellaneous possessions. Properties are mutually exclusive and multiply Happiness. Selecting a new property replaces the old one; clicking the current paid property returns to Homeless.

<CatalogTable kind="properties" />

Miscellaneous possessions can be activated independently and multiply their named channel.

<CatalogTable kind="items" />

Catalog costs are **daily recurring expenses**, not ordinary purchase prices. Bargaining, Intimidation, Brainwashing, Abyss Manipulation, and Galactic Command reduce those expenses. Great conversion has a separate one-time fee. If recurring expenses exhaust your copper, active paid possessions are cleared.

The expansion adds twenty possessions from Spaceship through The Unwritten Domain. Their unlocks combine copper with Dark Matter or Metaverse Point requirements where shown.

Eligible paid possessions, including the twenty expansion possessions, can receive permanent [Great conversions](../heroic/#great-item-conversions). Only Homeless is excluded. Properties and miscellaneous possessions follow separate ordered chains. Conversion costs one day of discounted Great upkeep and deactivates the item; check the fee and daily expense before confirming.

After the first Transcend, the [Shop Steward](../shop-steward/) can recommend a property and miscellaneous loadout. Its 10% income fee is an additional daily expense while enabled.
