---
title: Automation
description: Auto-promote, auto-learn, auto-pause, skips, and unlock retention.
category: systems
---

Automation appears at age 20 and remains unlocked after any rebirth.

- **Auto-promote** selects the next unlocked class in the current group.
- **Promote between careers** appears after the first Embrace Evil and allows auto-promote to scan forward through the complete class order, skipping locked classes rather than moving backward.
- **Auto-learn** chooses among unlocked abilities once per second. It ignores skipped abilities and abilities currently trained by an Echo. The Skip column excludes a specific ability without disabling Auto-learn or blocking manual selection.
- **Auto-pause before the Void** stops exactly at age 1,000, reduced by 100 years per Earlier Void rank.
- **Pause before Galactic Council** appears after the first Embrace Evil and stops exactly at age 10,000, reduced by 1,000 years per Earlier Galactic rank.

Clicking an ability row selects it manually, removes any Echo focus from that ability, and disables Auto-learn. Clicking only its Skip control leaves Auto-learn enabled. If every eligible ability is skipped or assigned to Echoes, Auto-learn waits instead of falling back to a skipped ability.

Automation toggles, ability Skip choices, and loadouts are saved and restored across application restarts. Loading a loadout disables auto-learn and auto-promote. Rebirth rules and feature eligibility still determine whether a saved choice can run.

:::note
Auto-pause follows the perk-adjusted unlock age and clamps a large time-warp step to that boundary, so it cannot skip past the milestone.
:::
