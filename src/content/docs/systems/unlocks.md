## Resource-gated groups

Dark Magic unlocks at 1 Evil. Almightiness unlocks at 1 Essence. Darkness abilities unlock from 3 through 10,000 lifetime Dark Matter. Dark Ascendancy classes unlock from 1 through 30,000 lifetime Dark Matter. Metaverse classes unlock from 1 through 100 lifetime Metaverse Points and use a sequential Great level-20 chain.

The [Chronicle](../chronicle/) contains 42 permanent milestones: nine Essence, fourteen Heroic, thirteen Darkness, and six Metaverse. [Essence Bargain](../evil-perks/) unlocks at 150,000,000 Essence.

Class, ability, and possession tables include their exact individual conditions. Requirements use inclusive thresholds: reaching the listed value is enough. Completed cached unlocks do not need to be re-earned unless rebirth logic explicitly clears them.

Mastery discovery is deliberately stricter than a class unlock: the class must actually complete a work tick. Selecting it without advancing work does not reveal the card. Its Copper through Ruby tiers depend only on the class's separately recorded worked peak; current levels and inherited task maximum levels do not count. See [Mastery Items](../mastery-items/) for every threshold and effect.
