Legacy contains 39 trainable abilities across seven groups. Only the selected ability gains XP.

Most effects are linear: `1 + coefficient × level`. Expense reductions use a capped logarithmic formula, Time Warping and Life Essence use logarithmic boosts, and Cosmic Recollection returns `level × 0.00065` for Transcend retention.

Dark Magic XP includes current Evil as a multiplier. Almightiness XP includes current Essence as a multiplier.
