Legacy contains 49 trainable abilities across nine groups. The selected primary ability gains XP, and up to four [Echoes](../echoes/) can train different abilities alongside it.

Most effects are linear: `1 + coefficient × level`. Expense reductions use a capped logarithmic formula, Time Warping and Life Essence use logarithmic boosts, and Cosmic Recollection returns `level × 0.00065` for Transcend retention.

Resonance I–IV have no direct multiplier. Each remembered lifetime peak sets its matching Echo's training efficiency, and an Echo cannot train its own Resonance.

The six Darkness abilities use `1 + coefficient × log10(level + 1)` and affect ability XP, Dark Matter, all XP, Essence, or Magic XP. They can become Great; Resonance abilities cannot.

Dark Magic XP includes current Evil as a multiplier. Almightiness XP includes current Essence as a multiplier.

Some [mastery items](../mastery-items/) add a small bonus to one named ability, an entire ability group, or all ability XP. All applicable mastery percentages are summed into one multiplier.
