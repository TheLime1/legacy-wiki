## What Legacy is

Legacy is a single-player incremental life simulation. You work one **class** and train one **ability** at a time while age advances. Class income pays the recurring expenses of your selected property and optional possessions. Abilities multiply specific parts of the economy or progression. Working classes also reveals permanent mastery collectibles with small passive effects. Later lives add permanent maximum-level bonuses, Evil, Essence, automation, shared leaderboards, and the Empire hierarchy.

## Eternal Ascension update

[Eternal Ascension](./systems/eternal-ascension/) expands the complete journey with four [Echoes](./systems/echoes/), permanent Evil perks, [Heroic and Great progression](./systems/heroic/), [Collapse and Darkness](./systems/darkness/), and [the Metaverse](./systems/metaverse/). The [Chronicle](./systems/chronicle/) tracks 42 permanent milestones and every reset through the Legacy Complete ending.
