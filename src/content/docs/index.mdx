## What Legacy is

Legacy is a single-player incremental life simulation. You work one **class** and train one **ability** at a time while age advances. Class income pays the recurring expenses of your selected property and optional possessions. Abilities multiply specific parts of the economy or progression. Later lives add permanent maximum-level bonuses, Evil, Essence, automation, shared leaderboards, and the Empire hierarchy.
